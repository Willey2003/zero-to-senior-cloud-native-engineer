# Getting Hired and Climbing to the Top Band

## Your public proof of work
- **GitHub**: this repo (with your journal and solutions), plus 3-5 polished projects with good READMEs and diagrams.
- **LinkedIn**: headline like "Linux | AWS | Kubernetes (CKA, CKS) | Platform & Cloud Security". Post weekly about what you built/broke/fixed.
- **Blog** (Hashnode/Medium/dev.to): one article per phase ("How I built container networking by hand").
- **Open source**: from month 12, contribute docs/tests to CNCF projects (Kubernetes, Kyverno, Argo, Backstage). Good first issues.
- **Community**: Kubernetes/CNCF meetups in your city, KubeCon India, speak at a meetup by year 2.

## Interview preparation
- **Linux/networking troubleshooting live**: practise `scenarios/` out loud.
- **System design**: design a multi-region platform, a CI/CD system, a zero-trust network, a secure multi-tenant K8s.
- **Coding**: Python scripting problems (parse logs, call APIs), basic data structures; for top companies, LeetCode easy/medium.
- **Behavioural**: STAR stories from your journal (an outage you fixed, an improvement you drove).
- **Salary**: always negotiate; research ranges (levels.fyi, AmbitionBox, Glassdoor); a competing offer is your best tool.
  Global remote roles (US/EU companies hiring in India) are where the 1 Cr+ packages usually are.
